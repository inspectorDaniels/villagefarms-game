## Verdict
This is the first module in the project that already looks like a game and not a prototype. The warm paper cards, Georgia numerals, brass-rimmed launcher buttons, pinned job notices and painted portraits all read as one indie HUD, and nothing about it feels like a web admin page. Over the bright meadow it reads well, and at night the cards stay legible. The weak point is layout. At 1600×900 the tall panels (Market, Finances) run down over the contextual prompt. At 1024×700 the centred sheet covers the minimap caption and slices through the toast stack. The toolbar also leaves an invisible strip that eats world clicks. The Market sheet is the one place where it falls back to a spreadsheet. The API matches the brief name for name, and degradation without simulation/terrain/roads is designed rather than broken. It passes, but the layout collisions need fixing in round 2.

## Must fix (ordered)
1. **Panels overlap the bottom-centre prompt at the target resolution.** `.hv-panel { max-height: calc(100vh - 118px - 150px) }` lets the sheet end at about y=782, while the prompt sits at y≈745–785. In market_1230.png / market_1930.png the "E — Hoe the verge" prompt is hidden under the Market sheet, and in finances_1230.png it pokes out underneath. Compute the max height from the actual top of the bottom-centre slot (or hide/raise the prompt while a panel is open) so that nothing is covered at 1600×900.
2. **1024×700 layout collides.** narrow_jobs.png, narrow_finances.png and narrow_land.png show the 700 px sheet covering the minimap's "Harvest Valley" caption and cutting the toast stack in half ("…nned on the jobs board."). Toasts must never sit under a panel. Either put `.hv-toasts` above `.hv-panelwrap` in z-order and move it clear, or clamp the panel width to the space between the side columns (`min(700px, 100vw - 2×(minimap width + gutter))`) and shift toasts below the panel's right edge. The README's own threshold of "< 1000 px" is not met at 1024.
3. **An invisible dead zone above the toolbar eats world clicks.** The `ui:toolbar` HUD node is `hv-hit`, 348×91 px, and includes the tool-name row plus the gap. Probes at (746,807) and (631,800), on transparent pixels beside the "Hoe" chip, returned `uiCapturing=true` and the canvas never received the click. Give the bare wrappers (`card:false` HUDs, `toolsWrap`) `pointer-events:none` and only mark the visible card, chip and prompt as `.hv-hit`.
4. **Declare the optional dependencies.** `manifest.optionalDeps: []`, but the module reads `simulation`, `environment` (via world), `terrain` and `roads` through `ctx.modules.get`. The brief lists them as the data sources and ARCHITECTURE §1 expects them in the manifest (boot also uses optionalDeps for init order). Set `optionalDeps: ['simulation','environment','terrain','roads']`. This is harmless to wave-1 isolation because they stay optional.

## Should fix
- **The `empty` preset is not empty when showcase deps load.** In empty_1230.png the live simulation is present, so Finances shows real data. The empty states are only visible with `uisolo=1` (solo_empty.png). Make the preset force the no-economy path (e.g. a data-adapter flag) so the critic view shows it without the solo hack.
- **The Market sheet reads like a spreadsheet** (market_1230.png): 13 identical rows, "None in store" repeated 13 times, and a ~150 px dead gap between the trend % and "Best at …". Wheat, barley and oats share one 16 px icon, so they are indistinguishable. Group items (grain / roots / animal produce), show stock only when it is > 0, bring "Best at" next to the price, and give barley and oats their own glyphs.
- **The balance chart overstates tiny moves** (finances_1230.png): three data points (starting "3 Feb", a day before the game began) are drawn as a smooth sigmoid on a €17,850–€18,000 axis, so a €139 drift looks like a crash. Enforce a minimum y-span (e.g. ±5 % of the balance), draw steps (the ledger is discrete) and do not plot days before game start.
- **The night treatment is timid** (default_2330.png, game_night.png, solo_night.png): the cream slabs glare against a near-black scene and the minimap stays full daylight green (brightness .84 only). Dim the paper more (lamplit, warmer, ~15 % darker), darken the minimap wash at night, and add a faint warm edge glow. Readability is fine now; the goal is for the HUD to sit in the night rather than on top of it.
- `uiCapturing` stays stale after a modal or panel closes by keyboard. After Esc on a confirm with the pointer resting over the Land sheet it reads `false` until the next pointermove. Re-evaluate with `document.elementFromPoint(lastPointer)` on finish/close.
- Panel hotkeys (J/M/O/P/H) and Space do not set `ev.stop`, so later key handlers also see them (a later handler recorded `KeyJd`). Stop them once handled, and ignore them when Ctrl, Alt or Meta is held.
- World labels are not clamped away from the HUD: "Kouter · ploughed" sits against the money card and is cut at the viewport corner at 1024 (narrow_default.png, top-left), and "Beemd · for sale" runs under the launcher. Hide or fade labels that intersect HUD cards.
- Showcase money-delta pill shows "+€1,396.04" while the toast says €1,535 (default_1230.png), because live rent is folded into the demo delta. This is cosmetic but visible in the showcase.
- Jobs cards duplicate the amount ("Plough 3.6 ha" + chip "3.6 ha"). Drop the chip when the title already contains it.

## What works
- The look is right: textured paper, ink numerals in Georgia, tinted wood/brass borders, soft drop shadows, kc key caps, and hand-drawn 24 px icons with slight wobble (zoom_topleft.png, zoom_bottom.png). The portraits in the character bar have charm, and the "Tab switch" affordance is a nice touch.
- The clock card is excellent: a sun/moon arc dial, the date "Tue 2 March, Year 1", temperature plus weather and season, and a segmented speed control that reflects its state.
- The Jobs board as pinned notices with red pins, a "1 day left" warning in red and green Accept buttons is exactly the right fantasy. Accept moves a job to "In progress 1" and toasts "Contract accepted" (i_jobs_accepted.png).
- The Land sheet's inked parcel map with hatching and a selected outline works well. Rent goes through the confirm dialog (Esc cancels and keeps the panel open; OK signs, updates the stats and toasts "Lease signed"). Buy is correctly disabled when unaffordable and shows a "you need €X more" toast (i_confirm.png, i_land_after_rent.png).
- Hotkeys all verified: J/M/O/P/H open and toggle, Esc closes, Space pauses/resumes, +/− and NumpadAdd step 1→3→10 and back down to pause, speed buttons work, toolbar digits and B select tools, a minimap click jumps the camera, and the wheel over a panel does not zoom the world.
- The toast stack caps at 5 (i_toastflood.png). Text is escaped (the prompt with `<img onerror>` rendered as text).
- Graceful degradation is real: in solo mode the money card hides, the weather card shows "Spring / Day", the minimap paints parchment, and the panels show illustrated empty states (solo_empty.png). In the full game with roads failed, ui ran at 0.08 ms with no errors.
- Perf is a non-issue (0.18 ms avg).
